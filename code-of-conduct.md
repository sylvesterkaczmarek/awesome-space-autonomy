# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project should be professional, constructive, and focused on improving the quality of the resource. Contributors should discuss technical disagreements on their merits, provide evidence where possible, and treat other participants with respect.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, spam, undisclosed promotional manipulation, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces controlled by the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other material that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

Concerns about conduct should be reported privately to the repository maintainer through the contact methods listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and discreetly as practical.
